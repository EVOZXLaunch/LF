Frequently Asked Questions

Do I keep full ownership of my token?

Yes. The owner address you set during deployment is the only address that can call owner-restricted functions on your token. You can transfer ownership or renounce it entirely at any time.

How is the deployment fee paid?

The Factory quotes a live fee in EVOZ (native) at the moment you review your deployment. A small buffer is added automatically to absorb normal price movement between confirmation and mining, so your transaction doesn't fail over a rounding difference.

Can I change my token's settings after deployment?

Fields such as taxes, limits and wallets are set at deploy time and become part of your token's immutable bytecode, the same way any audited token generator works. Review every field carefully on the Deployment Review screen before confirming.

What happens if my deployment transaction reverts?

LaunchFuture simulates your deployment before asking your wallet to sign, and decodes the exact on-chain error whenever the network returns one — instead of a generic "execution reverted" message — so you know precisely what to fix.
